Feature breakdown and curriculum design

Platform Blueprint

The platform behaves like a private physics university: diagnostic admissions office, adaptive professor, simulation lab, textbook librarian, exam hall, and research map.

AI Diagnostic Intake

The diagnostic is adaptive. It starts broad, finds the learner’s level, then branches into targeted questions that locate exact gaps.

Math Placement

Arithmetic fluency, algebra manipulation, function sense, trigonometry, calculus, linear algebra, differential equations, probability, and proof maturity.

Physics Intuition

Forces, energy, fields, waves, heat, uncertainty, spacetime intuition, units, approximations, and model selection.

Reasoning Profile

Symbolic reasoning, spatial visualization, abstraction tolerance, error recovery, persistence, and preferred explanation style.

Complete Curriculum

Each domain contains lessons, prerequisites, textbook references, derivations, simulations, assessments, and unlock conditions.

Arithmetic Foundations

Fractions, powers, units, ratios, scientific notation, dimensional thinking.

Algebra

Equations, functions, graphs, logarithms, exponentials, symbolic manipulation.

Trigonometry

Unit circle, radians, identities, waves, phase, amplitude, harmonic motion.

Calculus

Limits, derivatives, integrals, multivariable calculus, vector calculus.

Linear Algebra

Vectors, matrices, vector spaces, eigenvalues, eigenvectors, Hermitian operators.

Differential Equations

ODEs, PDEs, oscillators, Fourier methods, boundary conditions.

Classical Mechanics

Newtonian, Lagrangian, Hamiltonian mechanics, oscillations, angular momentum.

Electromagnetism

Fields, potentials, Maxwell equations, waves, radiation, boundary-value problems.

Thermodynamics

Heat, work, entropy, engines, equilibrium, thermodynamic potentials.

Statistical Mechanics

Microstates, ensembles, Boltzmann factors, partition functions, quantum statistics.

Special Relativity

Spacetime, Lorentz transformations, four-vectors, relativistic energy-momentum.

General Relativity

Tensors, curvature, geodesics, Einstein equation, black holes, cosmology foundations.

Quantum Mechanics

Wavefunctions, operators, Hilbert spaces, spin, hydrogen, perturbation, scattering.

Quantum Field Theory

Fields, creation/annihilation operators, path integrals, QED, renormalization ideas.

Particle Physics

Symmetries, Standard Model, quarks, leptons, gauge bosons, Higgs mechanism overview.

Condensed Matter

Crystals, reciprocal space, phonons, bands, semiconductors, superconductivity.

Computational Physics

Numerical integration, ODE/PDE solvers, eigenproblems, Monte Carlo, simulations.

UI and UX Architecture

The interface should feel like a futuristic physics university: serious, immersive, interactive, and confidence-building without being childish.

Home Dashboard

Shows roadmap, mastery graph, current lesson, weak topics, next practice set, streak, and exam readiness.

Lesson Workspace

Split view with explanation, derivation steps, AI tutor, simulation, notebook, and problem panel.

Knowledge Map

Zoomable dependency graph where locked, learning, mastered, and review-needed concepts are visually distinct.

Simulation Lab

Full-screen, parameter-rich labs with prediction prompts before running the simulation.

Exam Hall

Timed, proctored-style practice with rubrics, confidence tracking, and post-exam remediation.

Research Mode

Advanced mode that connects textbook knowledge to papers, open problems, derivations, and code notebooks.

Execution Plan

Build the smallest product that proves adaptive physics learning works, then expand into simulations, institutions, certification, and research-level learning.

MVP Plan

The MVP should be ambitious enough to prove the learning loop, but narrow enough to ship. Start with math-to-Quantum-I readiness.

Month 1

Foundation product

Auth, onboarding, diagnostic shell, concept graph schema, learner dashboard, and core design system.

Month 2

Adaptive curriculum

Arithmetic through linear algebra path, mastery tracking, first roadmap generator, and textbook summary structure.

Month 3

AI tutor and practice

Socratic tutor, hint ladder, generated quizzes, grading rubrics, weakness memory, and review scheduling.

Month 4

Physics core

Mechanics, waves, calculus-based problem sets, formula map, and first simulations.

Month 5

Quantum bridge

Modern physics, linear algebra-to-quantum bridge, particle-in-a-box lesson, tunneling visualizer, and readiness exam.

Month 6

Private beta

Invite learners, measure completion, tutor helpfulness, diagnostic accuracy, and exam readiness improvement.

MVP Success Criteria

Do not judge the MVP by content volume. Judge it by whether the adaptive loop changes learning behavior.

Diagnostic Validity

Learners agree with placement, and follow-up performance confirms detected gaps.

Learning Lift

Users improve on post-tests for targeted concepts after tutor + practice loops.

Retention

Learners return weekly because the next step is clear and the tutor is useful.

Problem Quality

Generated problems are correct, level-appropriate, and have useful rubrics.

Graph Utility

Unlocking and remediation feel accurate rather than arbitrary.

Trust

Users can see sources, assumptions, and why the AI recommends a topic.

Monetization

The business model should preserve a strong free learning path while charging for personalization, AI depth, certificates, and institution tooling.

Free

Static curriculum, limited diagnostic, limited AI messages, selected simulations, and public study roadmaps.

Pro Learner

Full AI tutor, adaptive roadmap, progress memory, practice generation, exam mode, and all core simulations.

Research Plus

Advanced tracks, paper assistant, computational notebooks, derivation checker, and high-limit AI tutoring.

Education License

Cohorts, instructor tools, SSO, LMS export, private content, analytics, certificates, and support.

Certification

Paid proctored certificates for calculus readiness, classical mechanics, quantum foundations, and research preparation.

Enterprise

Corporate quantum literacy training for engineering, finance, semiconductors, energy, and AI teams.

Premium AI

Higher tutor limits, deeper grading, custom projects, advanced simulations, and personal learning reports.

Launch Strategy

Launch around a specific transformation: “business or non-physics background to quantum-ready learner.” That is concrete and emotionally resonant.

Audience

  • Career switchers aiming for physics, quantum, or engineering programs.
  • STEM-adjacent professionals entering quantum computing.
  • Students who failed or feared physics the first time.

Go-To-Market

  • Public interactive diagnostic as lead magnet.
  • Free quantum-readiness report.
  • Weekly “from zero to quantum” challenge.
  • Partnerships with online STEM communities.

Content Engine

  • Short concept visualizations.
  • Before/after AI tutoring examples.
  • Problem-solving livestreams.
  • Public roadmap templates for different backgrounds.
Strong first wedge: “pre-master physics bridge for ambitious non-physics graduates.”

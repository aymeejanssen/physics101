Product Vision

The platform is not a video library. It is an adaptive physics university: diagnostic, curriculum, tutor, simulator, textbook guide, knowledge graph, assessment engine, and research bridge in one system.

Entry point Beginner

Arithmetic, algebra, functions, trigonometry, and study habits for learners without a physics background.

Core path BSc

Calculus-based mechanics, E&M, thermodynamics, statistical mechanics, relativity, and quantum mechanics.

Advanced path MSc

General relativity, quantum field theory, particle physics, condensed matter, and computational physics.

Learning engine AI

Diagnostic testing, Socratic tutoring, adaptive practice, memory of weaknesses, and mastery-based unlocking.

The Learning System

Every part of the product feeds the next: diagnostic results shape the roadmap, tutoring updates mastery, practice exposes gaps, and the graph unlocks the next concept only when the learner is ready.

Core Product Pillars

The website is designed as a serious, gamified-but-not-childish academic environment: a place where a motivated learner can move from zero to research literacy.

AI Diagnostic Intake

Adaptive intake measures arithmetic, algebra, calculus readiness, physics intuition, abstract reasoning, and preferred learning style.

  • Item response theory style difficulty adjustment.
  • Concept graph placement and prerequisite gap detection.
  • Personalized roadmap with time estimates.

Complete Curriculum

From arithmetic foundations to quantum field theory, particle physics, condensed matter, and computational physics.

  • Mastery gates between dependent topics.
  • Textbook-aligned chapter intelligence.
  • Exam and derivation checkpoints.

Adaptive Tutor

The AI tutor asks questions, generates hints, detects misconceptions, and remembers recurring weaknesses.

  • Socratic first, direct answer later.
  • Multiple difficulty levels per explanation.
  • Weakness memory and review scheduling.

Simulation Studio

Interactive simulations for mechanics, waves, electromagnetism, relativity, quantum mechanics, and particle physics.

  • Parameter sliders and real-time graphs.
  • Three.js/WebGPU for 3D scenes.
  • Python/WASM numerical kernels for advanced models.

Knowledge Graph

Every concept has prerequisites, dependencies, formulas, misconceptions, examples, and assessments.

  • Dynamic unlocking based on mastery.
  • Graph-based recommendations.
  • Search across concepts, books, derivations, and problems.

Practice Engine

AI-generated quizzes, derivation exercises, exam-style problems, conceptual questions, and challenge paths.

  • Hint ladders instead of instant answers.
  • Rubric-based written solution grading.
  • Olympiad and research-prep challenge modes.

Curriculum Spine

The curriculum is a dependency chain, not a menu. Learners can browse freely, but mastery unlocks serious progression.

Foundation

Arithmetic, Algebra, Trigonometry

Numbers, units, equations, functions, graphs, trigonometric waves, exponentials, logarithms, probability basics.

Mathematics

Calculus, Linear Algebra, Differential Equations

Derivatives, integrals, vector spaces, eigenvalues, ODEs, PDEs, Fourier transforms, tensors, complex numbers.

Classical Physics

Mechanics, E&M, Thermodynamics, Statistical Mechanics

Forces, energy, fields, Maxwell equations, entropy, ensembles, probability, and the classical models quantum theory replaces.

Modern Physics

Special Relativity, General Relativity, Quantum Mechanics

Spacetime, tensors, curvature, wavefunctions, Hilbert spaces, operators, spin, angular momentum, hydrogen, and perturbation theory.

Research Bridge

QFT, Particle Physics, Condensed Matter, Computation

Fields, Feynman diagrams, Standard Model foundations, crystals, band theory, superconductivity, simulation, and numerical physics.
